# Pre-event post templates

Use these examples as a starting point. Replace placeholders, check event details, and link to the **Forum event topic** so people can read the information and join the discussion. The Forum topic contains the link to Pretix for **RSVP**.

Forum event page: `{forum_event_url}`<br>
Pretix RSVP page: `{pretix_rsvp_url}`

```text
Wondering where the Ansible project is heading? Join us at the Ansible Community Meetup on {date} at {place}, {time} {time zone}. Details and discussion: {forum_event_url} RSVP: {pretix_rsvp_url}
```

```text
We are having an Ansible Community Meetup on {date} at {place}, {time} {time zone}. Join us and share your Ansible journey. Event details: {forum_event_url} RSVP: {pretix_rsvp_url}
```

```text
Learn how Ansible can help with your automation requirements. Join us at the Ansible Community Meetup on {date}, {time} {time zone}. Read the event topic and RSVP: {forum_event_url}
```

```text
Ansible {city} Meetup is happening on {date} at {venue}, {time} {time zone}. Learn about Ansible automation with the community. Details: {forum_event_url} Register via Pretix: {pretix_rsvp_url}
```

```text
It is time for Ansible Community {city} to meet again. Join us on {date} at {venue}, {time} {time zone} for talks and conversation. Check the Forum event topic for the agenda and RSVP link: {forum_event_url}
```
