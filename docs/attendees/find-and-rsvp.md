# Find an event and RSVP

## Find events

1. Browse the [Ansible Forum Events category](https://forum.ansible.com/c/events/8) for upcoming events.
2. To get notices for events near you, open your [Forum profile preferences](https://forum.ansible.com/my/preferences/profile) and set your **Event locations**.
3. You can also read the [Bullhorn weekly newsletter](https://forum.ansible.com/c/news/bullhorn/17) for community news and event announcements.

## Reserve a place

1. Open the event topic in the Forum. This is the event's main information page; check its date, time, venue or online details, and agenda.
2. Follow the RSVP link in the topic to Pretix. Production registration is at [rsvp.ansible.com](https://rsvp.ansible.com).
3. Sign in using your Forum account and complete the free registration.
4. Keep the confirmation email or ticket available for check-in if the event uses tickets.

The Forum topic links to Pretix, and the Pretix event links back to that topic. If you notice different event details, ask the organiser in the Forum topic; details are not synchronized between services.
