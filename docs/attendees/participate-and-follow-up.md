# Participate and follow up

## Before and during the event

- Read the Forum event topic for the agenda, venue or connection details, and updates.
- Reply to the topic to ask questions or suggest a talk. The organiser may use replies to shape the agenda.
- Follow the Ansible [Code of Conduct](https://docs.ansible.com/projects/ansible/devel/community/code_of_conduct.html) and respect the event's photography and accessibility guidance.
- Introduce yourself, share your experience, and help make space for other voices.

## After the event

1. Return to the Forum event topic to find the recap, slides, recordings, and follow-up resources.
2. Add constructive feedback or continue the technical discussion in the topic.
3. Share a talk idea or volunteer to help at a future event. You can find upcoming events in the [Forum Events category](https://forum.ansible.com/c/events/8).
